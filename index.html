Confirmed

What we've agreed with Commercial

Decisions from Wilfrid (Commercial Engine) and James Brown (Commercial Strategy) — September 2026

DecisionDetailSource
Customers: Early Life + In Life Renewal/OOC excluded. Renewal customers call about bill increases (downspin). OOC customers see bill reduction even on upgrade (negative revenue). In-life pricing is simpler — incremental £3-6 on existing contract. James Brown
MVP: Broadband upsell Broadband speed upgrade is the simplest starting point. Front book uplift is £6. Post-Kahlo: modular step pricing enables more flexibility. If the customer wants something more complex, transfer to human. James Brown + Wilfrid
Pricing: Range, not fixed Sierra gets a range (e.g. £3-6 for BB uplift) and decides within it based on customer tone, usage data, and value. NOT "one price, take it or leave it." Human agents go with deepest discount 99% of the time — Sierra can be smarter. Commercial test opportunity. James Brown
Stack: Voice, not digital Digital and voice are completely separate stacks with different offers and pricing. Build on Discover/RetainX (voice) so offers match what human agents see on escalation. Digital APIs (XSUS) confirmed as wrong path. Wilfrid
AI can use customer data Download/upload data, device counts, usage patterns — available but human agents never use it. Sierra should cross-reference with lifestyle questions for smarter recommendations. "I can see you've got 10 devices — let's recommend 500Mb." James Brown
Impact on the CX risk: The Early Life + In Life filter significantly reduces the 3,900 downspin calls — those are predominantly renewal/OOC customers. And pricing flexibility within ranges means Sierra can respond to "that's too expensive" without immediately escalating. Both decisions improve containment and CX together.

How Sierra Sells

The negotiation model — and how Kahlo enables it

Steps 1-2 confirmed with pricing range. Post-Kahlo modular pricing makes this work. Steps 3-5 stay with human agents.

#PositionWhat Sierra doesRevenueStatus
1 More for more Lead with the upgrade at a range price (e.g. £6). Use usage data + lifestyle Qs to justify value. Positive ARPU Confirmed
2 More for same If objection, flex within range (e.g. £3). "I can do that upgrade for £3 more." One reintroduction max. Neutral ARPU Confirmed
3Same for sameRecontract at current price — out of MVP scope (in-life customers less relevant) Not MVP
4Same for lessRetention pricing — human agents only Human
5Less for lessDowngrade — human agents only Human
Kahlo enables this (October 2026)
Modular L8 pricing replaces rigid L1 bundles. Each component priced independently with stackable discounts.

Broadband step pricing:
M125 £20 → M250 £24 → M350 £28 → M500 £32 → Gig1 £36 → Gig2 £40

Upgrade conversation: "£4-8 more per month" — Sierra picks within the range. Pre-Kahlo bundles service-protected 12 days post-launch.
The commercial opportunity
Today: human agents go with the deepest discount 99% of the time. Upselling actually loses ~£7 ARPU because discounts wipe out the gain.

With Sierra + Kahlo: AI leads with a higher price, flexes only if needed, and uses customer data to justify value. Smaller, proportional TLDs (<15%). Upsells that make money.

This is a test: does AI with data and pricing flexibility outperform human agents who default to max discount?

Still Open

What needs to be resolved before we can build

Two blockers and three open items. Target: Jan 2027 go-live.

Blockers

1. Regulatory steer on AI negotiation
Commercial has confirmed Sierra can have pricing flexibility. But can an AI legally vary price based on customer tone? Present a second option when the customer objects? This needs regulatory sign-off before any journey involving price goes live.
2. Integration path
Target architecture is RetainX CDO MSA (voice stack). But James Brown estimates RetainX is "at least 2 years away" (Maestro → Discover took 5-6 years and £20m+).

Sierra needs an interim integration path. Options:
• Consume MDM / Discover APIs directly (voice stack, not digital)
• Use digital in-life store APIs as a bridge
• Build a lightweight service layer that can migrate to RetainX later

Next step: Architecture session to decide interim path.

Open Items

3. Post-Kahlo offer catalogue mapping
Kahlo lands end of October. Which specific offers does Sierra present for broadband upsell? Need to map the new L8 product codes, pricing steps, and bundle options. Depends on Kahlo build completing.
4. Governance
Who signs off on Sierra's offer rules? Same governance as human agents, or separate AI approval? How often do rules change — dynamic or periodic refresh?
5. Volume sizing by lifecycle
DS to provide lifecycle breakdown within AddToPackage. What proportion of the 24,600 are early life + in life (Sierra's scope) vs renewal + OOC (excluded)? This confirms the addressable volume and whether 30% containment is realistic on the filtered pool.
