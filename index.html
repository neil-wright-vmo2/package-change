What we've agreed with Commercial

Decisions from Wilfrid (Commercial Engine) and James Brown (Commercial Strategy) — September 2026

DecisionDetailSource
Customers: Early Life + In Life Renewal/OOC excluded. Renewal customers call about bill increases (downspin). OOC customers see bill reduction even on upgrade (negative revenue). In-life pricing is simpler — incremental £3-6 on existing contract. James Brown
MVP: Broadband upsell Broadband speed upgrade is the simplest starting point. If the customer wants something more complex, transfer to human. James Brown + Wilfrid
Pricing: Range, not fixed Sierra gets a range (e.g. £3-6 for BB uplift) and decides within it based on customer tone, usage data, and value. NOT "one price, take it or leave it." Human agents go with deepest discount 99% of the time — Sierra can be smarter. Commercial test opportunity. James Brown
Stack: Voice, not digital Digital and voice are completely separate stacks with different offers and pricing. Build on Discover/RetainX (voice) so offers match what human agents see on escalation. Digital APIs (XSUS) confirmed as wrong path. Wilfrid
AI can use customer data Download/upload data, device counts, usage patterns — available but human agents never use it. Sierra should cross-reference with lifestyle questions for smarter recommendations. "I can see you've got 10 devices — let's recommend 500Mb." James Brown
Impact on the CX risk: The Early Life + In Life filter significantly reduces the 3,900 downspin calls — those are predominantly renewal/OOC customers. And pricing flexibility within ranges means Sierra can respond to "that's too expensive" without immediately escalating. Both decisions improve containment and CX together.

How Sierra Sells

The negotiation model

Steps 1-2 confirmed with pricing range. Commercial Engine API provides the offers and pricing. Steps 3-5 stay with human agents.

#PositionWhat Sierra doesRevenueStatus
1 More for more Lead with the upgrade at a range price (e.g. £6). Use usage data + lifestyle Qs to justify value. Positive ARPU Confirmed
2 More for same If objection, flex within range (e.g. £3). "I can do that upgrade for £3 more." One reintroduction max. Neutral ARPU Confirmed
3Same for sameRecontract at current price — out of MVP scope (in-life customers less relevant) Not MVP
4Same for lessRetention pricing — human agents only Human
5Less for lessDowngrade — human agents only Human
Commercial Engine provides the offers
Sierra consumes a Commercial Engine API endpoint — same model as online. Segmentation, pricing, and offer selection are hidden behind the API.

Sierra receives multiple offers with a pricing range and uses the conversation + customer data to select the best one for the customer.
The commercial opportunity
Today: human agents go with the deepest discount 99% of the time. Upselling actually loses ~£7 ARPU because discounts wipe out the gain.

With Sierra: AI leads with a higher price, flexes only if needed, and uses customer data to justify value. Upsells that make money.

This is a test: does AI with data and pricing flexibility outperform human agents who default to max discount?

The Build

Three things to prove — in sequence

Each phase proves one key capability before moving to the next. No point building pricing negotiation if customers can't do package discovery.

1

Can Sierra help customers discover the right package?

CE sends all eligible packages. Single fixed price per package — no negotiation. Sierra uses lifestyle questions and usage data to recommend the best fit. Customer objects to the price? Offer a different package, not a different price. Current package is the floor — never less than what they have today.

Customer: "I want faster broadband"
▶
Sierra: "Based on your 10 devices, I'd recommend M500 at £38/mo"
▶
Customer: "Too much" → Sierra offers M350 at £34
2

Can Sierra contain the full journey end-to-end?

Customer agrees to a package. Sierra handles everything: billing impact, compliance disclosures, CIS/CSS to email, verbal consent, order to ICOMS, QuickStart booking, confirmation email, CC Notes. Customer walks away with a completed package change — no human involved.
